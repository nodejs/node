Our Story


We are a team of developers who love technology but hate seeing it go to waste. Every year, millions of perfectly usable or recyclable smartphones end up in landfills, releasing toxic chemicals into the environment.


We built EcoMobile to create a frictionless way for people to pass on their old devices. Whether you want to get some cash back for your old phone, or donate it to someone who needs digital access, we use tech to make the process as simple as possible. No hidden fees, no complicated processes—just smart code and a greener future.
